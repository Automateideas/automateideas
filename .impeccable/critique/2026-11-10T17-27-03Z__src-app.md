## Design Specificity Verdict
Auth pages are authored (two-tone headline, brand-tinted icon chips, tilted product preview). The signed-in app is default shadcn grey: swap the logo and it could be any SaaS. Brand green appears only as buttons and active icon.
Detector: CLI 0 findings. Browser: nested-cards on every page (mostly false positive: card header/footer slots and the login illustration; partly real: grey shell > white rounded panel > cards > alert = 3-4 container layers), overused-font (Instrument Sans 100%, acceptable for app UI), low-contrast 4.3:1 on auth showcase paragraph (#737373 on #f5f5f5, real), cramped-padding on tab list (false positive).

## Priority Issues
- [P1] Mobile menu open/close are an svg and a div with onClick (src/components/ui/sidebar.tsx). Not buttons, no label: keyboard and screen-reader users can't open navigation. Fix: real <button aria-label aria-expanded>. /impeccable harden
- [P1] Navigation duplicates and mislabels: sidebar Customers, Sales, Reports are placeholders while Billing has its own Customers, Reports, Payments tabs; "Billing" (your invoices) vs "Subscription" (what you pay us) is easy to confuse. Fix: one home per concept; hide unbuilt modules or mark them; rename Subscription to "Plan & seats". /impeccable clarify + distill
- [P1] Subscription overview contradicts itself: Starter plan, Trying Pro badge, Early access status, Monthly total ₹0 next to ₹500/month. Fix: one status sentence ("Free during early access, 5 seats; ₹500/month once payments start"). /impeccable clarify
- [P2] Weak hierarchy inside cards: card title 16px medium, description nearly same size; every card adds a grey sentence. Fix: stronger titles, cut restating descriptions, tighten 4 container layers to 2. /impeccable typeset + layout
- [P2] App lost the auth pages' character; dashboard is empty except a reminder. Fix: reuse brand-tinted icon chips (green/amber/red) on Billing overview and nav, dashboard greeting with workspace name/logo and 3-4 quick actions. /impeccable colorize + onboard
